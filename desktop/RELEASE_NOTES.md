**A true desktop overlay, rebuilt the Dussehra Garland way.** The kandil, string lights and diya float over your apps while you keep working underneath.

- Clicks pass straight through to the app below, except on the kandil, diya, bulbs and resize handles.
- The overlay never takes keyboard focus and installs **no keyboard or mouse hooks** and **no global shortcuts**: typing, Alt+Tab, Ctrl+Shift+Esc and every other shortcut work exactly as normal. It stays on top the ordinary way, so Task Manager and other windows you bring forward still come to the front.
- Brush the lights and the kandil with your pointer; click to switch the kandil, bulbs and diya; drag to swing the kandil, pull hard to pluck it; drag the diya; drag a corner handle to resize.
- A click in any app leaves a sparkle and a double-click drops a cracker on the taskbar (turn off in the menu).
- **Options and Quit:** right-click the kandil or diya, or right-click the tray icon near the clock.
- Smaller and lighter than before (Python + Qt instead of Electron).

Phones and any browser, no install: https://arunkw.github.io/diwali-lantern/

**Windows:** download `DiwaliLantern-<version>.exe` and run it. It is built by GitHub Actions from the public source, with `SHA256SUMS.txt` and a build-provenance attestation. SmartScreen may warn because the exe is unsigned: **More info → Run anyway**.
